Why Check the Entire Day?

Anthropic's official incident record says users across Claude.ai, Claude Code, and other surfaces were erroneously required to use usage credits for an included model.

At least one affected account later received a correction limited to 18:16-18:47 UTC, but its six invoices dated July 17 total $599.35. The customer never claimed that the problem lasted only 30 minutes. A full-day review is necessary before treating a narrow automated credit as complete reconciliation.

Evidence Collection

  1. Save every invoice or receipt dated July 17.
  2. Save the matching card-statement entries.
  3. Save Anthropic's incident-correction email and note its selected time window.
  4. Export or capture plan-usage indicators and reset times if available.
  5. Record when extra usage and auto-reload were disabled.
  6. Record whether Claude Code continued working under included plan limits afterward.
  7. Preserve the complete support conversation and its conversation ID privately.
  8. Record each support message's date, read state, responder type, and promised action.

Transaction-Level Questions for Anthropic

For every July 17 debit, ask Anthropic to identify:

  1. the exact timestamp;
  2. the model and surface involved;
  3. the applicable included-plan entitlement and remaining allowance;
  4. the event that routed usage to paid credits;
  5. the resulting usage-credit debit;
  6. the auto-reload threshold and trigger event;
  7. the invoice or receipt created; and
  8. the correction, refund, or reason Anthropic claims the charge was valid.

Distinguish These Outcomes

  • Refund to original payment method: money returned through the original payment rail.
  • Account credit: a balance usable only with Anthropic, potentially expiring.
  • Ledger correction: reversal or adjustment of an incorrect usage-credit debit.
  • Explanation only: not a financial correction.
  • Narrow incident-window credit: may leave the rest of July 17 unreconciled.

Public-Safe Reporting

Helpful aggregate facts:

  • invoice count and total billed;
  • number and total of auto-reloads;
  • amount and form of any correction;
  • whether included-plan work continued after extra usage was disabled; and
  • whether a human billing owner completed a full-day audit.

Do not publish: invoice numbers, support IDs, account email, card or bank details, private screenshots, one-time codes, payment links, or complete private support transcripts.
