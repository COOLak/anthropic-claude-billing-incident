What Anthropic Billing Platform should inspect to resolve this.

Owner Routing Request

Please route the affected support case to the Anthropic owner who can inspect Claude usage-credit purchase state across checkout, payment confirmation, invoice/order commit, discount eligibility, and credit-ledger provisioning. Latest support posture now points toward specialized billing-team review; a fresh mailbox sweep at 2026-07-26 20:11 JST found no new human billing-owner response or resolution. The missing step is a confirmed human owner and resolution.

Current Handoff State

A direct human connection has been requested after support acknowledged that the contradiction needs specialized billing-team investigation. A fresh mailbox sweep at 2026-07-26 20:11 JST found no new human billing-owner response or resolution. The case remains unresolved until Anthropic confirms the owner/team, explains the failure, and restores the manual prepaid-credit path or applies an equivalent adjustment.

Observed Good Path

  • Works Automatic usage-credit top-ups continue charging successfully.
  • Works The account can be billed through the existing billing setup.

Observed Bad Path

  • Fails Manual prepaid usage-credit purchases fail or lose advertised discounted checkout state.
  • Unclear Support has not explained whether failed checkout state affected discount eligibility, invoice/order state, or ledger provisioning.

Inspect These Systems

  1. Manual usage-credit checkout session creation and handoff state.
  2. Payment confirmation / 3D Secure completion state for failed manual attempts.
  3. Order or purchase-commit records created after payment confirmation.
  4. Invoice or billing document records connected to failed manual attempts.
  5. Discount-cycle and advertised-discount eligibility state before and after failed manual attempts.
  6. Usage-credit ledger provisioning records and any delayed or reversed credit entries.
  7. Auto-reload billing path records for the same account, to compare why automatic top-ups can charge while manual prepay fails.
  8. Spend-limit and extra-usage evaluation records, including cases where CLI prompts tell users to buy credits while a spend cap or included-plan quota is the real blocker.
  9. Model-access / entitlement flags for Fable and other plan-included models, especially when Claude Code routes users to usage-credit pages despite visible plan quota remaining.
  10. Default-model resolver and command/skill frontmatter override path, especially where model or effort selection silently changes cost tier or routes users to usage-credit gates.
  11. Credential precedence and stored billing-source state, including environment tokens, API keys, stored primary API keys, and subscription login state.
  12. Support-visible account and organization mapping, especially where Fin or support sees a Free/API organization while user-facing Claude surfaces show a paid plan.
  13. Plan-change, cancellation, refund, and entitlement-reversal state, especially where automated support flows acknowledge manual review but cannot route a human billing owner.
  14. Support-routing history to determine why the user received generic card-decline guidance instead of Billing Platform / payment-operations review.

Minimum Useful Reply

A useful reply from Anthropic would include one of the following:

  • A restored working manual prepaid usage-credit purchase path at the advertised discounted terms.
  • An equivalent credit or discount adjustment applied directly by Anthropic.
  • A written Billing Platform / payment-operations explanation that reconciles the manual prepay failure with successful automatic top-ups.
  • Written confirmation that failed or stuck manual checkout state did not consume discount eligibility, create hidden payment debt, or penalize the account.

What Would Not Resolve It

  • Generic advice to update a payment method.
  • Generic advice to contact the issuing bank.
  • Pointing to automatic top-ups as proof of resolution.
  • Closing or deflecting the support thread without identifying a Billing Platform / payment-operations owner.
