Independent-verification gap

Anthropic's current help article says usage credits are the continuation mechanism after included limits are reached, customers should see a notification when the session limit is reached, the Usage dashboard clearly distinguishes included-plan usage from paid-credit consumption, and included limits reset every five hours without credits changing the reset timing.

The public record does not yet contain a stable event-level key joining the account-visible state to the payment chain. That is the decisive independent-verification gap—not the existence or amount of the two payments.

Minimum independently reviewable packet

A sanitized, timestamped capture could test the account-visible part of the chain if it showed the applicable included-limit buckets immediately before and after reset; any notification that Claude says authorizes switching to usage credits; the credit balance immediately before and after a named Claude Code request or bounded session; and the next matching recharge notification with private identifiers redacted.

Even that packet would not prove Anthropic's server-side classification by itself. Anthropic must disclose a safe reconciliation key—or a signed mapping using its internal identifiers—that joins the request, entitlement decision, paid-credit debit, recharge trigger, invoice, payment, and any correction.

Records only Anthropic can supply

  1. Effective subscription entitlement and every applicable included-limit bucket.

  3. The model or feature-specific gate Anthropic believes remained exhausted.
